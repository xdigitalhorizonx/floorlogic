Capability schedule

Software that knows what a roll is.

Generic field-service apps can schedule a crew — they can’t price a seam. Every line below is specced the way we needed it on our own jobs.

5.01
Roll-goods math

Cut cost and roll cost per square yard, priced per product, so the waste lives in the quote instead of coming out of your margin. Materials and labor separate on every line, tax by county, good / better / best options the customer picks from.

5.02
Hosted quotes & online approval

The quote is a live link with versioning, expiration, and view tracking — you know who opened it and when. Approval is recorded with a timestamp and emails the signed PDF to your rep.

5.03
Work orders & scheduling

Written by the approval itself. Crews see their schedules, job photos, and tasks on any phone — and a job can split across two installers when it needs to.

5.04
Invoices & card payment

The deposit invoice follows your payment terms and takes cards right on the link — collected the minute they approve, not 45 days after the floor goes down. Stripe’s standard rate; FloorLogic adds nothing on top.

5.05
Job costing

Estimate vs. actual vs. variance on every job — contractor payments, purchases, supplies — each cost with a receipt photo attached. Know what you made before the van is unloaded.

5.06
Inventory & purchase orders

On-hand, allocated, prepared, available, shortages, and low-stock alerts that match the warehouse — with POs tracked from ordered to received.

5.07
Sample checkouts

Which board went home with whom, and when it’s due back — so displays stop walking away.

5.08
The record remembers

Two-way texting lives on the job record — when someone says “that’s not what we agreed to,” you scroll, you point, you’re done. Calls are logged with recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries, and Casper, the built-in assistant, digs answers out of your own records.

5.09
Books with a PIN on them

Bank feeds, reconciliation, auto-categorization, payables and receivables, commissions — gated to a finance allowlist, so an installer can cost a job without ever seeing the books.

Requests for information

The questions we’d ask, too.

What happens after I enter my email?

A real person builds your private workspace and emails your login — usually the same day. No credit card, no sales call required. FloorLogic is single-tenant, so a human sets up each company’s own database rather than a signup script. We think that beats a “book a demo” form by a mile, and we’d rather tell you exactly how it works than pretend there’s a magic instant-signup behind the button.

Is my data mixed in with other companies’ data?

No. Every company runs in its own private database. Your quotes, customers, and books are not rows in a shared system sitting next to your competitor’s.

Will my crew actually use it?

It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet, or desktop — nothing to install, no server to babysit. Crews see their work orders, schedules, job photos, and tasks. The accounting section is PIN-gated to a finance allowlist, so an installer can cost a job without ever seeing the books.

Does it replace QuickBooks?

FloorLogic has its own books: bank transaction import with reconciliation and auto-categorization, payables and receivables, job costing, and Stripe payout reports. There’s no two-way QuickBooks sync today — we’d rather say that here than surprise you in week two. Landmark Flooring runs entirely on FloorLogic’s books; if you need to keep QuickBooks, tell us about your setup and we’ll give you a straight answer before you commit.

Is there a mobile app?

FloorLogic is a responsive web app — it runs in the browser on phones and tablets, with nothing to install and no app-store updates to chase. We say that plainly because you should know exactly what you’re getting.

How long is the trial, and what does it cost after?

14 days, free, no credit card. After that it’s $149, $299, or $549 a month depending on tier — the full pricing is right on this page. Annual billing gets two months free. There is no setup fee.
