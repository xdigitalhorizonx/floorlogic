## What the build takes from this
- One main action: start the free trial; secondary: "Take the tour" / watch the 45-second video.
- Email-first form, more fields only after commitment.
- Real, clickable product in the first screen (our living board).
- Plain words (grade 5–7), short sections; let the modules do the talking.
- Prices on the page; dated competitor facts; honest proof only.
- Phone-first, fast (zero third-party requests).
